# Ixos for Schools — Business / Rollout Roadmap (manual plan)

This roadmap is for **you** to execute manually: meetings, pilots, policy, pricing, and launch coordination. It follows your launch notes themes (students speak, teachers speak, board culture, ethical statement, interviews, test groups, A/B testing, long reflections, financing, tracking user).

## Positioning (what you’re selling)

### Core promise
**Appropriate AI for appropriate tasks** — aligned with your school’s culture and curriculum.

### What you should *not* say
- “ChatGPT replacement” (sounds like a toy and triggers fear).

### What you should say
1) **Teacher-edited School Soul (PRIVATE)**: culture + curriculum alignment.
2) **Study Modes**: tutor/marker/quiz/flashcards that teach better learning.
3) **Student Coach**: helps students use AI properly.
4) **Budget controls**: predictable spend and transparency for leadership.

---

## Track A — Stakeholder discovery (get real requirements)

### A1) Students (adoption + hooks)
Collect:
- top 5 use cases they do weekly
- what makes them switch tools (speed, attachments, UI, “feels smart”)
- fears (hallucinations, getting caught, wasting time)

Outputs:
- Ranked “Top 10 student tasks” list
- Minimum Delight Set (the features that create switching behavior)

### A2) Teachers (subject agents + boundaries)
Collect:
- what resources they’ll upload (slides, booklets, mark schemes)
- acceptable vs unacceptable help (integrity boundaries)
- how they want students guided

Outputs:
- 3–5 teacher agent templates (Physics / English / Econ / French / TOK)
- A short “AI boundaries” statement teachers agree on

### A3) Leadership / Barry / Board (permission + constraints)
Collect:
- privacy and retention red lines
- budget expectations
- branding requirements
- any documentation required

Outputs:
- 1-page leadership brief: Policy + Privacy + Budget controls
- Pilot approval checklist

---

## Track B — Ethics + privacy stance (turn trust into a feature)

### B1) Student-friendly ethical statement
Include:
- what data is stored (messages, analytics, memory)
- what is *not* visible to students (School Soul raw text, other students’ data)
- how citations work
- opt-in proactive nudges (deadlines)

### B2) Attachment retention policy (important)
Decide defaults that leadership won’t fear:
- personal attachments auto-delete after X days unless pinned
- derived text + embeddings can be kept longer
- teacher pack resources kept until teacher deletes (or policy)

Outputs:
- In-app “Privacy & Data Use” page
- Short policy summary used in onboarding

---

## Track C — Pilot design (prove value quickly)

### C1) Cohorts
Start small:
- DP1 pilot + a few teachers
- expand to DP2 + TOK once stable

### C2) Test groups + A/B testing
Pick 2–3 comparisons:
- General chat vs Study Modes
- Prompt enhancer ON vs OFF
- Auto routing vs Manual

### C3) Feedback loops
- weekly micro-feedback (5 questions)
- occasional long-form reflection

### C4) Success metrics (undeniable)
- weekly active users
- productive sessions (Tutor/Marker/Quiz/Research)
- attachment success rate
- teacher trust score
- cost per active student

Outputs:
- 1-page pilot plan
- “How we measure success” dashboard view

---

## Track D — Teacher enablement (adoption engine)

### D1) Teacher Playbook (in-product)
- build a subject agent fast
- what to upload for best results
- how to guide students ethically
- how to interpret analytics

### D2) Soul workshop (short teacher session)
- agree on tone and boundaries
- collect curriculum hints
- publish School Soul v1

Outputs:
- School Soul v1
- 3 teacher agents published for students

---

## Track E — Financing & pricing (keep flexible)

### E1) Pitch “budget control”, not “price per student”
Your system can enforce:
- org budgets
- spending tiers
- fallbacks to free models

So your leadership pitch:
- “You set the budget; Ixos stays inside it.”

### E2) Premium access for all, controlled by orchestration
Frame it as:
- everyone can access premium models
- router uses them only when needed
- caps prevent runaway cost

Outputs:
- leadership cost explainer
- scenario sheet (low/medium/high adoption)

---

## Track F — Marketing + pitch assets (keep it professional)

### F1) Narrative
- “The school’s AI tutor — aligned with our values and curriculum.”
- “Teaches students how to learn and how to use AI properly.”
- “Transparent, safe, and cost-controlled.”

### F2) Assets
- 1-slide overview
- 3-minute demo script (student workflow + teacher workflow)
- short FAQ (privacy, cheating, costs, citations)

---

## Track G — Launch operations (smooth rollout)

### G1) Onboarding flows
- Student onboarding:
  - choose goal
  - show 3 suggested modes
  - show prompt enhancer example
- Teacher onboarding:
  - Soul editor
  - create agent
  - upload resources

### G2) Support workflow
- one bug/feedback channel
- known issues list
- process for communicating fixes

### G3) Iteration rhythm
- weekly improvements
- monthly Soul + teacher template refresh

---

## High-leverage order (what to do first)
1) student + teacher discovery interviews
2) Soul workshop + teacher templates
3) pilot with clear metrics
4) leadership brief (privacy + budget + pilot results)
