## Findings grouped by root cause

### W1-14-F01 — Invalid verification stub remained beside valid fixtures

- Consequence: a future investigator could mistake `P1V3-essay.docx` for a valid Office fixture and misdiagnose Word/bridge behavior.
- Severity: small operational risk. Confidence: high.
- Evidence: historical `P1-truth-engine.md:199` and `STATE.md:116-123`; pre-move size 942 bytes, SHA-256 `5CCBF396...C59A331` (full restore manifest below).
- Remedy: completed reversible archive of the whole folder, including the stale Word lock file. No permanent deletion.

### W1-14-F02 — Contaminated scratch output had no lifecycle marker beyond its name

- Consequence: `P5V1-Exercises-contaminated-001411.docx` could be reused accidentally or obscure the intended control/fresh/identity fixture set.
- Severity: small operational risk. Confidence: high.
- Evidence: direct-child inventory found only that file, 17,644 bytes, no Git/reparse marker and no active/runtime/source reference.
- Remedy: completed reversible archive of `spectra-p5v1-docx-scratch`.

### W1-14-F03 — Downloads fixture retention has no machine-readable owner/expiry manifest

- Consequence: names such as `final`, `mounted`, `fix`, `control`, `fixture` and `corpus` are insufficient to distinguish an active mount, a reusable regression corpus and stale scratch. Aggressive cleanup would risk removing valuable Office/bridge evidence.
- Severity: future operational. Confidence: high.
- Evidence: 10 candidates were retained for distinct historical/fixture reasons; `spectra-p5v1-fix` additionally contains a primary-node-modules junction and old runtime logs. The current cleanup needed source history and process checks to classify them safely.
- Bounded remedy: maintain a small owner/role/last-validated/expiry manifest for future cleanup, and move reusable fixtures under the task-local `.spectra-local\launch-audit\fixtures` tree when practical. Do not delete retained corpora as part of this audit.

### W1-14-F04 — One focused source test depends on an absolute Downloads path

- Consequence: moving or cloning the RC mounted corpus breaks `xlsx-run3-materialize-diagnosis.test.ts` outside this machine.
- Severity: future test portability. Confidence: high for the source reference; runtime use is not implied.
- Evidence: `<local-workspace>` hardcodes `<local-workspace> Data Collection.xlsx`.
- Bounded remedy: parameterize the fixture root or copy the fixture into the task-local fixture area. This task intentionally did not change application/test source ownership.
