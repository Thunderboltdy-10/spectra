- Unified ordinary XLSX import, fresh export, and linked-workbook edits through the native `@ariadng/office` lens and one projection/delta path.
- Extended the workbook model with stable worksheet part identities, lifecycle/order/visibility/tab metadata, dimensions, hidden rows and columns, views, formula-cache state, calculation mode, active sheet, defined names, tables, and charts.
- Added native OOXML operations for worksheet lifecycle and axis edits, cell/style/merge/freeze changes, workbook properties, defined names, tables, and chart parts. - Preserved untouched package parts, including unknown extensions, comments, diagrams, media, pivots, and macro bytes. Unsupported validation/conditional-format authoring and complex chart families remain explicit preserve/refusal paths.
- Routed the existing Sheets command/capability surface through these operations, including native table removal, and removed the unreachable legacy ExcelJS XLSX importer.

## Unresolved material limits

- Combo and histogram chart families are refused by the safe native writer; unmodeled chart XML is preserved rather than synthesized.
- Validation and conditional-format rules are read and preserved, but their native authoring remains Excel-owned.
- Pivots, comments/notes, macros, external links, and unknown extensions remain opaque preserved parts; macros are never executed.
- Row/column structural edits are limited to one line per operation. Worksheet deletion refuses when native formulas, chart references, names, or validation formulas still point at the target.
