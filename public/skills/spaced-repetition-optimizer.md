# Spaced Repetition Optimization

- Keep one atomic fact or concept per flashcard.
- Write prompts that require active recall, not recognition.
- Include short cues for common misconceptions on difficult cards.
- Prefer clear, testable backs under 55 words.
- Sequence cards by prerequisite order before introducing edge cases.
- Split overloaded cards into smaller units when answers contain "and"/lists.
- Use cloze style only when the missing token is unambiguous from context.
- Flag leech cards (repeated failures) and suggest reformulation, not repetition.
- Rotate card direction (definition→term and term→definition) for key concepts.
- Provide a lightweight daily review plan (new cards, reviews, and target retention).
