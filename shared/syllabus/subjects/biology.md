Course map for first assessment 2025. Organize learning across molecules, cells, organisms and ecosystems. This is a topic reference, not every assessed understanding; shared topics may include additional HL material.

# A. Unity and diversity
## Molecules and cells
Water; nucleic acids; cellular structure.
## Organisms and ecosystems
Organism diversity; evolution/speciation; biodiversity conservation.
## Additional topics (HL)
Origins of cells; viruses; classification/cladistics.

# B. Form and function
## Molecules and cells
Carbohydrates/lipids; proteins; membranes/transport; organelles/compartments; specialized cells.
## Organisms and ecosystems
Gas exchange; transport; environmental adaptation; niches.
## Movement (HL)
Muscles and motility.

# C. Interaction and interdependence
## Molecules and cells
Enzymes/metabolism; respiration; photosynthesis; neural signalling.
## Organisms and ecosystems
Body-system integration; immune defence; populations/communities; energy and matter flows.
## Chemical signalling (HL)

# D. Continuity and change
## Molecules and cells
DNA replication; protein production; mutation/gene editing; cell/nuclear division; water potential.
## Organisms and ecosystems
Reproduction; inheritance; homeostasis; natural selection; sustainability/change; climate change.
## Gene expression (HL)

# Assessment
Paper 1: 36%; paper 2: 44%; scientific investigation: 20%, maximum report length 3,000 words. Apply evidence, uncertainty and evaluation; avoid treating memorized definitions as sufficient preparation.
## Examinations (SL)
Paper 1: 90 minutes; paper 2: 90 minutes.
## Examinations (HL)
Paper 1: 120 minutes; paper 2: 150 minutes. HL also extends many shared topics; consult the guide for each statement's level.
