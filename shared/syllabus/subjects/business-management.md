First assessment 2024. Use this edition's briefs: some IB web-page prose still describes the previous course. Connect decisions to change, creativity, ethics and sustainability. Topic names below are a compact map, not all assessment requirements.

# 1. Business foundations
Entities, objectives, stakeholders, growth and multinational organizations.
# 2. People at work
Human-resource management, structure, leadership, motivation and communication.
## Organizational culture and employee relations (HL)
# 3. Finance
Funding, costs/revenue, accounts, profitability/liquidity, cash flow and investment appraisal.
## Debt/equity ratios and budgets (HL)
# 4. Marketing
Planning, research and the seven-P mix.
## Sales forecasts and international marketing (HL)
# 5. Operations
Methods, location and break-even decisions.
## Extended operations (HL)
Lean production/quality; production planning; contingency/crisis planning; R&D; management information systems.

# Toolkit
Select tools that answer the decision in the case. Check the guide's level allocation; do not prescribe every available tool for every answer.

# Assessment
Both levels complete a business research project, maximum 1,800 words, using a conceptual lens. Link evidence to the specific organization; avoid generic recommendations.
## Papers and IA (SL)
Paper 1: 90 minutes, 35%; paper 2: 90 minutes, 35%; IA: 30%.
## Papers and IA (HL)
Paper 1: 90 minutes, 25%; paper 2: 105 minutes, 30%; paper 3: 75 minutes, 25%; IA: 20%. Paper 3 addresses a social enterprise.
