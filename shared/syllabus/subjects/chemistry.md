First assessment 2025. Connect models of structure with explanations of reactivity. This compact map omits fine-grained understandings: shared topics contain level-specific content, so check the linked guide before declaring an advanced treatment SL.

# Structure
## S1. Particle models
S1.1 Matter and particles; S1.2 nuclei/atoms; S1.3 electron configurations; S1.4 moles; S1.5 ideal gases.
## S2. Bonding and materials
S2.1 ionic; S2.2 covalent; S2.3 metallic; S2.4 models applied to materials.
## S3. Classification
S3.1 periodicity; S3.2 organic functional groups.

# Reactivity
## R1. Energetics
R1.1 measured enthalpy changes; R1.2 energy cycles; R1.3 fuels.
### R1.4 Entropy and spontaneity (HL)
## R2. Amount, rate and equilibrium
R2.1 reaction quantities; R2.2 kinetics; R2.3 extent of reaction.
## R3. Reaction mechanisms
R3.1 proton transfer; R3.2 electron transfer; R3.3 electron sharing; R3.4 electron-pair sharing. Not all detail in these topics is shared by SL and HL.

# Assessment
Paper 1: 36%; paper 2: 44%; scientific investigation: 20%, maximum 3,000-word report. Practise experimental interpretation alongside calculations. Distinguish observations, models and assumptions.
## Examinations (SL)
Paper 1: 90 minutes; paper 2: 90 minutes.
## Examinations (HL)
Paper 1: 120 minutes; paper 2: 150 minutes. Additional depth occurs throughout structure and reactivity, beyond R1.4.
