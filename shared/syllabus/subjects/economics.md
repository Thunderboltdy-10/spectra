First assessment 2022. Interpret models using evidence and their assumptions. The nine concepts are scarcity, choice, efficiency, equity, well-being, sustainability, change, interdependence and intervention. This map does not enumerate each level-specific understanding.

# 1. Foundations
Economic questions, methods and the problem of resource allocation.
# 2. Microeconomics
Demand/supply, equilibrium, elasticity, intervention and market failures, including externalities, public goods and common resources.
## Additional microeconomics (HL)
Asymmetric information, market power and firms' market structures; further quantitative depth.
# 3. Macroeconomics
National activity, aggregate demand/supply, objectives and policies, distribution, demand management and supply-side approaches.
# 4. Global economy
Trade, protection, integration, exchange rates, balance of payments, sustainable development and development strategies.
## Additional global-economy work (HL)
Terms of trade and further quantitative/policy analysis; confirm the guide's precise level boundaries.

# Assessment
IA: three news-based commentaries, maximum 800 words each, using different units and different key concepts. Apply diagrams and evidence to the article; do not merely summarize it. Check article eligibility and dates against the current guide.
## Papers and IA (SL)
Paper 1: 75 minutes, 30%; paper 2: 105 minutes, 40%; IA: 30%.
## Papers and IA (HL)
Paper 1: 75 minutes, 20%; paper 2: 105 minutes, 30%; paper 3: 105 minutes, 30%; IA: 20%. Paper 3 includes policy recommendations.
