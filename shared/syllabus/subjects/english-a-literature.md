For the Language A literature route, first assessment 2021. This is a compact reference; get the student's actual reading list before choosing texts or quotations.

# Reading and interpretation
Study literary craft, readers' interpretations, historical/cultural contexts and relationships between texts. Trace how choices create effects; avoid replacing analysis with plot summary.
# Assessment
Paper 1 analyzes unseen literature; paper 2 compares two studied works. The oral connects two literary works through a global issue, including a work in translation.
## Assessment (SL)
Paper 1: 35%; paper 2: 35%; oral: 30%.
## Assessment (HL)
Paper 1: 35%; paper 2: 25%; oral: 20%; HL essay: 20%, 1,200–1,500 words on a studied work. HL has a larger reading programme.
