Beginner English, SL only; assessment 2020–2028. This outline does not substitute for the language-specific syllabus.

# Themes and topics
Identities: attributes, relationships, food/drink, physical well-being.
Experiences: routine, leisure, holidays, festivals.
Human ingenuity: transport, entertainment, media, technology.
Social organization: neighbourhood, education, workplace, social issues.
Sharing the planet: climate, geography, environment, global issues.
# Communication
Use short, achievable listening, reading, writing and speaking tasks in English. Scaffold vocabulary and sentence construction, then gradually remove support.
# Assessment
Writing: 25%; listening: 25%; reading: 25%; individual oral: 25%. The oral uses a visual stimulus, discussion and a conversation across themes. There is no HL route.
