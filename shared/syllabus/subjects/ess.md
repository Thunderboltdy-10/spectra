# Additional lenses (HL)
Environmental law; environmental/ecological economics; environmental ethics. Apply these lenses across topics and include the additional HL understandings, not just a separate revision block.

# Assessment
Use case studies and data to connect causes, consequences and management decisions. The investigation uses a report of at most 3,000 words. The practical programme includes a collaborative sciences project.
## Papers and IA (SL)
Paper 1: 60 minutes, 25%; paper 2: 120 minutes, 50%; investigation: 25%.
## Papers and IA (HL)
Paper 1: 120 minutes, 30%; paper 2: 150 minutes, 50%; investigation: 20%.
