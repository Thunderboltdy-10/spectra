For assessment from 2027; no SL/HL. This is a research process reference, not a subject-specific guide. For examinations ending in 2026, obtain the earlier guide instead of applying the revised criteria.

# Pathway and research question
Choose subject-focused or interdisciplinary research using approved DP subjects. Establish scope, feasibility, disciplinary methods and a question that can be answered with available evidence.
# Research and argument
Record source details, evaluate evidence and connect each analytical step to the question. Revise the scope when the evidence warrants it; distinguish findings from unsupported assumptions.
# Structure
Title page, contents, introduction, analytical body, conclusion and references/bibliography. The essay limit is 4,000 words. Check the guide's detailed inclusion/exclusion rules before counting.
# Reflection
Three formal supervisor meetings, ending in a viva voce. Submit a final reflective statement of at most 500 words on the Reflection and progress form. Discuss decisions, learning and transfer rather than a task diary.
# Assessment
Revised criteria: framework; knowledge/understanding; analysis/argument; discussion/evaluation; reflection. Do not use the previous focus/method, critical-thinking, presentation and engagement rubric. Supervisor deadlines and subject-specific requirements take precedence over a generic checklist.
