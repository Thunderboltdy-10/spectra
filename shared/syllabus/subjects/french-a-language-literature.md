For the Language A language-and-literature route, first assessment 2021. Distinct from literature-only and Language B. Retrieve the school's works and non-literary bodies of work before giving text-specific advice.

# Texts and meaning
Analyze literary and non-literary communication through form, audience, purpose and context. Explore interpretation, time/place and connections between texts. Support each claim with precise textual evidence.
# Assessment
Paper 1 uses unseen non-literary material; paper 2 compares two studied literary works. The oral links literature and a non-literary body of work through a global issue.
## Assessment (SL)
Paper 1: 35%; paper 2: 35%; oral: 30%.
## Assessment (HL)
Paper 1: 35%; paper 2: 25%; oral: 20%; HL essay: 20%, 1,200–1,500 words on a studied work/body of work. HL includes a larger reading programme.
