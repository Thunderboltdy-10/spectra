Literature and performance in French; SL only, first assessment 2024. This is an interdisciplinary literature/theatre course. Confirm the school's authorized language offering and chosen works.

# Interpretation and performance
Connect close reading with staging decisions. Explore audiences, cultural contexts and relationships between written and performed texts. Keep rehearsal notes tied to evidence from the work.
# Assessment
Comparative essay examination: 30%; written assignment: 30%; transformative performance and individual oral: 40%. The assignment analyzes the student's performance choices in a dramatic extract. The transformative task stages non-dramatic literature.
# Study guidance
Describe what an audience sees and hears, explain its textual basis and evaluate alternatives. Retrieve the student's own performance evidence before making specific claims about it.
