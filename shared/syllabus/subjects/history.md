For examinations through 2027. This is the most recent history edition within the first-assessment-2027 cutoff used by this catalogue. Confirm the school's chosen options before planning.

# Prescribed subject
Choose one: military leaders; conquest; move to global war; rights/protest; conflict/intervention.
# World-history topics
Choose two. Available areas include medieval societies/wars/rulers; early-modern transitions/states/wars; industrialization; independence; democratic states; authoritarian states; twentieth-century wars; Cold War. Confirm the school's topics and required examples from different regions.
# Regional depth study (HL)
One regional option, with three sections: Africa/Middle East, Americas, Asia/Oceania or Europe.
# Historical investigation
IA maximum 2,200 words: source identification/evaluation, investigation and reflection. This is the older investigation structure.

# Assessment
Practise source analysis separately from comparative essays. Build arguments around evidence and evaluate interpretations; a chronological narrative alone is insufficient.
## Papers and IA (SL)
Paper 1: 60 minutes, 30%; paper 2: 90 minutes, 45%; IA: 25%.
## Papers and IA (HL)
Paper 1: 60 minutes, 20%; paper 2: 90 minutes, 25%; paper 3: 150 minutes, 35%; IA: 20%.
