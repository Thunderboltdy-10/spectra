First assessment 2021; current assessment through 2028. AA emphasizes reasoning and analytical fluency with and without technology. This is a compact map; use the guide's SL/AHL statement numbers for exact boundaries.

# 1. Number and algebra
Scientific notation, sequences/series, financial applications, logarithms/exponentials, binomial expansion and proof.
## Further algebra (HL)
Induction and contradiction; complex numbers; further counting, series and algebra.
# 2. Functions
Domains/ranges, inverses/composition, transformations, quadratics, rational/exponential/logarithmic models and solving equations.
## Further functions (HL)
Polynomial structure, further rational functions, modulus and inequalities.
# 3. Geometry and trigonometry
Measurement, radians, circular functions, identities and trigonometric equations.
## Vectors and further trigonometry (HL)
Vector geometry, lines/planes and additional identities.
# 4. Statistics and probability
Data, correlation/regression, probability, discrete random variables, binomial and normal models.
## Further probability (HL)
Conditional probability/Bayes and continuous random variables.
# 5. Calculus
Differentiation/integration, graph behaviour, optimization, area and motion.
## Further calculus (HL)
Advanced methods, differential equations and Maclaurin expansions.

# Assessment
Exploration: 20%; criteria assess presentation, communication, engagement, reflection and mathematics.
## Examinations (SL)
Paper 1: 90 minutes, 40%, no calculator; paper 2: 90 minutes, 40%, technology required.
## Examinations (HL)
Paper 1: 120 minutes, 30%, no calculator; paper 2: 120 minutes, 30%, technology required; paper 3: 60 minutes, 20%, technology required.
