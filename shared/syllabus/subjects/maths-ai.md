For assessment in 2021–2028. AI emphasizes modelling, statistics and technology-supported interpretation. It is distinct from AA and from discontinued Mathematical Studies. This map does not enumerate every SL/AHL statement.

# 1. Number and algebra
Approximation, sequences/series, financial applications, logarithms and numerical techniques.
## Further algebra (HL)
Complex numbers and matrices, including applications.
# 2. Functions
Representations, modelling, fitting, solving and interpreting functions in context.
## Further models (HL)
More advanced function models and transformations.
# 3. Geometry and trigonometry
Measurement, trigonometry, bearings, coordinates and Voronoi diagrams.
## Vectors and graphs (HL)
Vector methods, graph theory and network applications.
# 4. Statistics and probability
Sampling, descriptive statistics, correlation/regression, distributions and hypothesis tests.
## Further statistics (HL)
Additional distributions, inference and tests. Confirm assumptions and the guide's test requirements before choosing a method.
# 5. Calculus
Rates, optimization, integration and numerical approximations.
## Further calculus (HL)
Differential-equation models, Euler approximations and further integration.

# Assessment
Exploration: 20%, with presentation/communication/engagement/reflection/mathematics criteria. Make assumptions and model limitations explicit.
## Examinations (SL)
Paper 1: 90 minutes, 40%; paper 2: 90 minutes, 40%. Technology required in both.
## Examinations (HL)
Papers 1 and 2: 120 minutes each, 30% each; paper 3: 60 minutes, 20%. Technology required throughout.
