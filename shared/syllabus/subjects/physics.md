# Assessment
Paper 1: 36%; paper 2: 44%; investigation: 20%, report at most 3,000 words. No separate options paper. Integrate uncertainty, experimental design and evaluation into practice.
## Examinations (SL)
Paper 1: 90 minutes; paper 2: 90 minutes.
## Examinations (HL)
Paper 1: 120 minutes; paper 2: 150 minutes. Shared C.1/C.3/C.5, D.1/D.2 and E.1/E.3 also have HL extensions.
