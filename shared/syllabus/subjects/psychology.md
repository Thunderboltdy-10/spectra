For examinations from 2027. This replaces the earlier options-based course and experimental-replication IA. Integrate concepts, content and contexts rather than learning isolated study summaries.

# Concepts
Bias, causality, change, measurement, perspective and responsibility.
# Content
Biological, cognitive and sociocultural explanations of behaviour, with research methodology.
# Contexts
Health/well-being; human development; human relationships; learning/cognition. All four are studied, with class practicals integrated across them.
# Class practicals
At least four. Practise design decisions, ethics, interpreting findings and evaluating claims. Retrieve the class's actual practicals rather than inventing what the student did.
# Extensions (HL)
Culture, motivation and technology as influences on behaviour; analysis and interpretation of research data.

# Assessment
IA: a research proposal for a specified population; not conducting the previous course's replication. Use evidence to justify method and ethical choices.
## Papers and IA (SL)
Paper 1: 90 minutes, 35%; paper 2: 90 minutes, 35%; IA: 30%.
## Papers and IA (HL)
Paper 1: 90 minutes, 25%; paper 2: 90 minutes, 25%; paper 3: 105 minutes, 30%; IA: 20%.
