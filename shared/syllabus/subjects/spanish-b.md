Language acquisition for learners with prior experience; assessment 2020–2028. This is a course map, not a language-specific grammar specification.

# Themes
Identities; experiences; human ingenuity; social organization; sharing the planet. Topics within these themes are suggestions, not a prescribed checklist.
# Communication
Practise listening, reading, writing and conversation in Spanish. Match register and text type to the situation; explain corrections and let the student retry.
# Assessment
Writing: 25%; listening: 25%; reading: 25%; individual oral: 25%.
## Oral and writing (SL)
The oral starts from a visual stimulus; writing tasks target SL proficiency.
## Literature and oral (HL)
Study two works originally written in Spanish. The oral begins with a literary extract; expect greater linguistic complexity.
