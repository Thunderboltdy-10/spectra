DP core course, without SL/HL. Ask how knowledge is produced, justified and limited. This is a compact reference; session-specific essay titles must be retrieved separately.

# Core theme
Knowledge and the knower: examine your assumptions and communities of knowers.
# Optional themes
Study two: knowledge and technology, language, politics, religion or indigenous societies. Establish which themes the class chose.
# Areas of knowledge
History, human sciences, natural sciences, arts and mathematics. Compare methods and standards of evidence without reducing an area to a stereotype.
# Knowledge questions
Connect claims to concrete examples, examine contrasting perspectives and explain implications. Keep discussion about knowledge rather than drifting into a general subject essay.

# Assessment
## Exhibition
Three objects tied to one prescribed prompt; combined commentary maximum 950 words; 33% of TOK. Make each object's particular context matter to the justification.
## Essay
Maximum 1,600 words on one of six session-specific prescribed titles; 67%. Use the exact title issued for the student's examination session. Do not substitute a remembered or invented title.
