# Citation styles

Use the style the user or their school asks for. Fill in only details the source actually shows;
leave out a missing element rather than inventing it.

## APA (7th edition)

- In text: (Author, Year) or Author (Year). Two authors: (Smith & Lee, 2021). Three or more:
  (Smith et al., 2021).
- Web page: Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). *Title of page*. Site Name. URL
- Journal article: Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. *Journal Name,
  Volume*(Issue), pages. https://doi.org/...
- Book: Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of book* (Edition). Publisher.

## MLA (9th edition)

- In text: (Author page), e.g. (Smith 42).
- Artifacts Cited entry: Author Last, First. "Title of Page." *Site Name*, Day Month Year, URL.
- Book: Author Last, First. *Title of Book*. Publisher, Year.

## Chicago (notes and bibliography)

- Footnote: First Last, *Title of Book* (Place: Publisher, Year), page.
- Bibliography: Last, First. *Title of Book*. Place: Publisher, Year.
- Web: Last, First. "Title of Page." Site Name. Month Day, Year. URL.

## Harvard

- In text: (Author Year, p. page), e.g. (Smith 2021, p. 12).
- Reference: Author, A. (Year) *Title*. Edition. Place: Publisher.
- Web: Author, A. (Year) *Title of page*. Available at: URL (Accessed: Day Month Year).

## IB extended essay notes

Any consistent recognised style is accepted. Every quotation, paraphrase, image and data set
needs a citation, and the bibliography lists only works cited in the essay.
